# Financial Governance

All monetary outputs are economic/model outputs. They are not accounting provisions, recognized liabilities, audited losses or guaranteed returns.

Loss components carry IDs to detect potential double counting. Regulatory monetary figures, where modeled, are treated as statutory ceilings or bounded scenario terms rather than expected penalties unless an actual empirical calibration supports otherwise.

Modeled ROSI = (avoided EAL − investment cost) / investment cost.
